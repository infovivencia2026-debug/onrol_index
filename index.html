FAQs

Questions students ask first.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Programs start from zero, and 70–75% of the time is hands-on with a mentor present.

Are sessions live or recorded?

Live, in small cohorts. Every session is recorded afterwards for review.

How much time per week?

The 21-day Accelerator is an hour a day. AI Generalist is three months with 50+ live hours; SOC Analyst is three months with 60+. Most people study alongside a job or college.

What do I walk away with?

Deployed work under your name, with live links for employers or clients. Proof, not a certificate.

Which program should I start with?

New to AI: the 21-day Accelerator. Career change: AI Generalist or SOC Analyst. Already building and want to lead systems: AI Architect. The skills quiz can recommend one.

How do I join, and what does it cost?

Apply on any program page. The team calls you back with fit, cohort dates and fees. No payment is needed to apply.
